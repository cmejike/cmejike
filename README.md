I’m a PhD student in Epidemiology (minor in Biostatistics) at the University of Arizona. Because I started in biotechnology, I care a lot about where data come from: who collected the samples, how they were handled, and what happened in the lab.

I work on the [Arizona CoVHORT study](https://covhort.arizona.edu/), a population-based cohort following people through and after COVID-19, with a focus on the gastrointestinal effects of Long COVID (LC). As a [clinical research coordinator](https://publichealth.arizona.edu/directory/chidera-ejike) for CoVHORT-GI, I manage participant eligibility and study group assignment, and I coordinate the preparation, tracking, and shipment of biospecimens. I’m especially interested in how the gut microbiome relates to LC recovery after infection.

Other work I’m involved in:

LC and food insecurity: my first paper as lead author (preprint)
Social support and resilience among people with LC in Arizona CoVHORT
A photovoice study on greenspace and health in Southern Arizona
A Fred Hutch study at the Uganda Cancer Institute on delays in antibiotic treatment for neutropenic fever
Outbreak investigation with the [SAFER](https://publichealth.arizona.edu/sites/publichealth.arizona.edu/files/SAFER.pdf) team

I’m a BIO5 Graduate Fellow and was a [Roots for Resilience Fellow with CyVerse and the Data Science Institute](https://wees.arizona.edu/person/chidera-ejike). Before grad school, I taught DNA barcoding and PCR to secondary-school students in Nigeria. I still enjoy explaining things more than almost anything else in research.

Most of my code is in R (tidyverse, ggplot2, gtsummary, tidymodels), usually in Quarto. Right now I’m learning metagenomic analysis pipelines and methods for integrating multi-omic data.
